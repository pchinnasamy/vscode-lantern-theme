# Changelog

## 1.2.0

- New light themes: Lantern Nightshade Light, Lantern Obsidian Light and Lantern Abyss Light. Their backgrounds are near-white with a hint of the matching dark theme's hue.
- In the light themes, gold splits in two: dark amber for the cursor, active line number, modified files and popup borders, and honey for badges, buttons, selection and find matches.
- Light themes have their own diagnostic, git and diff colors, tuned for light backgrounds.

## 1.1.0

- Badges, buttons (including Install and Update in the Extensions view) and popup borders now use each theme's highlight gold instead of blue or violet.
- Notification info icons and links use the gold too.
- Tabs, the activity bar indicator and focus borders keep the blue or violet accent.

## 1.0.0

- First release: Lantern Nightshade, Lantern Obsidian and Lantern Abyss.
